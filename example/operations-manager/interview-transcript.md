# Simulated gap interview — §5, per-role metrics

Answers written as a real warehouse ops manager would plausibly give them.

---

**Q: For Operations Manager at Cascade Distribution — what changed because you
were there? Ideally a number: a percentage, a count, a dollar figure, time saved.**

> I mean, I ran the warehouse. Things ran smoother, I guess. Hard to put a
> number on it.

**Q (second ask, concrete): Can we put a number on that? Even a rough one you'd
defend in an interview — before and after, how many people it reached, how long
something used to take.**

> Okay — when I took over we were shipping late constantly. Something like one
> in five orders went out past its promised date. It's rare now, maybe one in
> fifty. And the inventory thing: we used to shut down for a full physical count
> every month, two days, everybody on it. I moved us to cycle counting — couple
> hours a week, no shutdown.

**Q: What did you personally own versus the team?**

> The cycle-count switch was mine — I built the schedule and sold it to the GM.
> The late-shipment fix was me and my two leads.

**Q: How big was the team?**

> Fourteen across two shifts. I hired nine of them.

---

**Q: Shift Supervisor, 2016–2019 — same question.**

> Second shift had the worst safety record of the three. We went from a
> recordable incident every couple months to about eighteen months clean before
> I moved up. I ran the pre-shift briefings and rebuilt the pick paths so people
> weren't crossing forklift lanes on foot.

---

**Q: Warehouse Associate, 2013–2016 — anything, or should we keep it short?**

> Keep it short. I picked orders. I did get certified on the forklift and ended
> up training new hires on it.

---

## What the two-ask design produced

Three roles, zero numbers on the original resume → **six defensible metrics**,
none invented:

- late shipments 1-in-5 → 1-in-50
- monthly 2-day count shutdown → 2 hours/week cycle counting
- team of 14 across 2 shifts; hired 9
- worst-of-3 safety record → ~18 months incident-free
- rebuilt pick paths to separate foot traffic from forklift lanes
- forklift certified; trained new hires

The first answer to every question was vague. **Every number came from the
second ask.** A single-pass interview would have captured none of them.
