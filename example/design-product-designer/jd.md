# Senior Product Designer — Meridian Freight (example)

> A fictional job posting, used to show what `/job-hunt-os:apply` works from.

**Company:** Meridian Freight
**Location:** Remote (Canada) · HQ Toronto
**Salary:** $125,000 – $150,000 CAD
**Source:** example.com/jobs/senior-product-designer

## About the role

Meridian builds software for mid-market freight brokers. Our users are
dispatchers and operations managers who live in our product eight hours a day
and know their job far better than we do.

We're looking for a senior product designer to own the operations surface —
the dense, high-frequency screens where our users actually spend their time.

## What you'll do

- Own end-to-end design for our operations product, from research to shipped UI
- Work directly with dispatchers; we expect you to spend real time watching them work
- Contribute to and extend our design system
- Partner with engineering closely — we value designers who can read and write front-end code

## What we're looking for

- 6+ years designing B2B software, ideally operational or data-dense tooling
- Evidence of measurable outcomes, not just shipped screens
- Comfort with research: you can run your own sessions and synthesise them
- Design systems experience
- Bonus: logistics, supply chain, or payments background; front-end skills
